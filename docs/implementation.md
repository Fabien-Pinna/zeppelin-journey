This rebuild replaces the rejected first playable at `f9d17a3`. The user supplied three concept images and required a different stylized avatar, a completed/harmonized zeppelin and the full career rather than two grouped experiences. Visual acceptance remains with the user.

## Runtime

React 19 / R3F / Drei / Three / Vite, with exact versions in the lockfile. The scene loads nine Draco-compressed Blender dioramas, an original articulated traveller and an adapted passenger zeppelin. Decoder files are served locally. All physical scenery is actual 3D; the generated day/evening cloud imagery is only the distant background. Asset provenance and regeneration notes: [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md).

`world.js` owns nine destinations, narrative content, boarding anchors and ship berths. `Journey.jsx` owns walking, takeoff, free/guided flight, assisted landing and the damped camera. Berths sit beside the pier; boarding tests use the pedestrian anchor. Keyboard, mouse orbit, touch and gamepad adapters are retained. `Life.jsx` animates all nine activities on a shared simulation clock. A nearby guide opens a three-topic native conversation from `conversations.js`; only the current island needs a guide label. Exported movable glTF pivots animate the windmill, sheep and clock.

`App.jsx` provides compact departure copy, nine-stop navigation, day/evening controls, help and reduced motion. `Journal.jsx` uses a native focus-trapped dialog. `scripts/build-carnet.mjs` builds the independent no-JS/no-WebGL text edition from the same nine chapter definitions, preventing another reduced-content fallback.

Fourteen tests cover all chapters and models, Draco exports, both costumes after Three.js name sanitization, safe boarding/guide approaches, substepped collisions, sliding, corrected post placement, five-minute pendulum stability/contact clearance and frame-rate independence. Production build passes. Browser QA checks the long guided route to the warehouse, keyboard movement and boarding distance, all nine journal entries, Escape/focus restoration, day/evening and desktop/mobile layouts. See `QUALITY_LOOP_REPORT.md` for visual corrections and evidence.

`physics.js` consumes the per-object `layouts.json` manifest generated by Blender. A vertical capsule approximation resolves boxes/cylinders in short horizontal substeps, with island limits, gravity/jump, floor heights and a camera obstruction check. Moving sheep and the porter have time-aligned colliders. Plants and tiny decorative details are non-solid. Props are fixed, not pushable rigid bodies.

A fixed-step driven pendulum uses gravity and damping. Ropes, bar and acrobat share its transform; raised arm pivots keep grips on the bar. The second costume removes the backpack. Body poses and juggling trajectories are procedural, not motion capture. Conversation text uses verified career facts; the guides are fictional representatives. No audio or CMS was added. Physical multitouch/gamepad devices and low-end GPU performance remain unverified. Twelve GLBs total about 26 MB.
